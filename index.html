    Meet Our Founder

    Saloni Gourh

    Saloni Gourh, founder of Seemani, started the brand with a simple idea, beautiful accessories shouldn’t come with a heavy price tag.

    With a deep love for jewellery and styling, she created Seemani to offer hair accessories that look luxurious, feel premium, and remain affordable. Every piece is thoughtfully curated to combine elegance, durability, and comfort, so anyone can elevate their look effortlessly.

    At Seemani, the goal is clear: top-notch quality, timeless designs, and prices that never burn your pocket.
